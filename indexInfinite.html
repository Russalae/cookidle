How to play?

Welcome to Cookidle! Inspired by LoLdle and Wordle, your goal is to find who's today's cookie!

In Daily Mode, the cookie changes every 24 hours.
In Infinite Mode, the cookie changes every time you find one. Your goal is to get the highest win streak!

Color of the tiles changes depending on how close you are from today's cookie:
Green means the property is the same ;
Orange means the property is partially the same ;
Red means the property is not the same.

Please note that Cookidle is exclusively for Cookie Run Kingdom.
Guests and special cookies are included!

Properties

Gender
Based on Cookie Run Kingdom's English translation.
Possible values: She, Her, He, Him, They, Them, It, Its

Role
The cookie's role, their function in battle.
Possible values: Ambush, Bomber, Charge, Defense, Healing, Magic, Ranged, Support, BTS.

Position
Where the cookie stands in a team.
Possible values: Front, Middle, Rear.

Element
The attack type of the cookie. Some magic candies can add element to a cookie, they are included in the cookie's properties!
Possible values: Light, Poison, Fire, Water, Earth, Ice, Electricity, Darkness, Wind, Steel, Grass, Chaos.

Rarity
The rarity of the cookie.
Possible values: Common, Rare, Special, Guest, Epic, Super Epic, Dragon, Legendary, Ancient, Beast, Witch.

Release
The version the cookie was released.
Possible values: from 1.0 to 7.2

Clues

In Daily mode, clues will help you find the cookie of the day!

Quote
After 5 tries, you unlock the cookie's quote.
Each quote was chosen by hand to be not too obvious and can be from anywhere!
For example, it can be a skill quote, a tree of wishes quote, a kingdom quote... really pretty much anything, except from story mode!

Skill
After 10 tries, you unlock the cookie's skill.
First, it will be rotated and in greyscale.
After 15 tries, the skill will be the right way round.
After 20 tries, the skill will be in colors.
